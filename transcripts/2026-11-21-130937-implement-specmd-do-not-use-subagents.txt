  Three findings worth your attention

  mainly in rate (2–293 Hz). 13% of clustered samples burst, but they are
  spread across 84 of the 102 clusters rather than concentrated — a v(t)
  spectrum ties a burst train and a tonic train at the same fundamental to
  nearly the same comb. Chattering lands in a 17 Hz "slow tonic" cluster at
  membership probability 0.49. This is exactly what §18 item 9 predicts, and the
  delta-train spectrum it defers is the fix. The --spectral-tilt substitute was
  swept and lost.

  Adaptation is unmeasurable as specified. adapt_index uses the first and last
  ISI inside [500, 2500) ms, but adaptation is a transient that §6 discards — so
  +adapting never fires on any cluster. Measuring it needs one extra in-kernel
  accumulator across the transient.

  MULTIMODAL at this granularity is a sampling statement, not a finding. 69 of
  102 clusters trip it, but their largest parameter-space component holds ~5%
  across ~80 components: a 115-point cluster on a 3-D surface is simply sparser
