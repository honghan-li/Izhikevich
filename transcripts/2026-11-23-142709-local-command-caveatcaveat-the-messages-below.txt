  The headline

  The embedding isn't a taxonomy of types — it's a bifurcation diagram of the 
  Izhikevich reset map. The two super-structures are separated by the orbit 
  period of the ISI sequence:

  ┌──────────────────────────┬────────────────────┬─────────────────────────┐
  │                          │        blob        │         neuron          │
  ├──────────────────────────┼────────────────────┼─────────────────────────┤
  │ p = 1 (one ISI, repeated │ 99.47%             │ 0 of 15,895             │
  │  forever)                │                    │                         │
  ├──────────────────────────┼────────────────────┼─────────────────────────┤
  │ median I_max/I_min       │ 1.000              │ 2.4 – 64                │
  ├──────────────────────────┼────────────────────┼─────────────────────────┤
  │ return map               │ one point on the   │ 2, 3, 4… points, or a   │
  │                          │ diagonal           │ filled curve            │
  └──────────────────────────┴────────────────────┴─────────────────────────┘

  Only 85 of 48,095 blob samples have ISI contrast above 1.2; no neuron-side
  sample is below it. That's why they're topologically disconnected rather than
  just far apart — feature block B collapses to a single delta for every
  period-1 train.

  The cause is the reset voltage c, gated by d. Shallow reset (c ≳ −52) means
  the next spike arrives before u has relaxed, the +d kicks accumulate, and the
  fixed point period-doubles. c > −52 ∧ d > 1.5 gets 89% accuracy; c alone has
  AUC 0.936.

  The neuron's branches are the cascade

  With β = d/(c+62) (ρ with orbit period = −0.80): β≈1 → dendrite T (weak
  doublet, just past the bifurcation) → β≈0.6 axon (full doublet) → β≈0.35
  dendrite C (the period-3 window, 98.8% pure) → β≈0.15 dendrite B / the onion
  (period 4–16, the onion's rings are the orbit lengths) → β<0.1 chaos. (c,d)
  pick the orbit; a picks the tempo (ρ(log a, log rate) = 0.75–0.89 within every
  region). b is uninformative everywhere.

  Your cell-body observation is right and I could quantify it. It's 53%
  aperiodic, its return map is a filled unimodal curve, and 41% of its samples
  have spike-train spectral flatness > 0.10 against ≤8% everywhere else. All 57
  samples in the entire run with log-ISI entropy > 2.2 lie inside the soma box.

  Constraints

  A four-scalar classifier (orbit period, rate, ISI contrast, entropy) recovers
  the regions at 97.9% accuracy, seven of nine at F1 ≥ 0.93. Parameter-space
  boxes are given with measured hit/recall — they top out at ~95% for
  neuron/blob and ~87% nine-way, so they're proposal distributions, not
  definitions.

  Three things worth knowing:

  - The axon-terminal fan is an artefact. BJ and BY each consist of exactly one
  spike count (23 and 15 ISIs); the fan is HDBSCAN resolving integer counts in a
  2000 ms window, not distinct dynamics.
  - The great blob's 47 clusters are rate bins (η² = 0.83). Its width is purely
  the onset transient — within fixed rate bins, ρ(onset-ISI ratio, UMAP x) =
  +0.95.
  - Six of the seven canonical types are period-1 and therefore all in the blob.
  Only CH is on the neuron (period-5, correctly placed in the onion).
